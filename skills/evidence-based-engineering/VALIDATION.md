### Foundational Anti-Fabrication Principles
This skill establishes the core evidence-based engineering standards:

1. **No Fabricated Metrics**: Explicitly prohibits inventing success rates or performance scores
2. **Evidence Requirements**: All claims must be traceable to observable facts
3. **Uncertainty Expression**: Mandates appropriate hedging and skepticism
4. **Prohibited Language**: Defines banned superlatives without evidence

### Cross-Skill Impact
As the foundational skill, its principles are enforced across:

- distributed-systems-debugging: Ensures factual debugging reports

## Known Limitations

1. **Principles-Based**: Provides guidelines rather than automated enforcement
2. **No Executable Components**: Philosophy and methodology, not tooling
3. **Human Judgment Required**: Cannot automatically detect all forms of fabrication
4. **Context-Dependent**: Some domains may require adapted evidence standards
5. **Training Dependency**: Effectiveness depends on consistent application

## Special Note

This is the foundational anti-fabrication skill. It establishes the evidence-based engineering principles that underpin the entire Sartor Public Claude Skills Library. All other skills reference and implement these standards.
